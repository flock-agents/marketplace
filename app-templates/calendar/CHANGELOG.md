# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: one page, a month or week calendar of events (title, start, optional end, all day, notes). A picked event opens
  under itself in the day's list (month) or the week's list; Add opens under the page header, under the picked day, or under the week.
  Replaces the old Family Calendar marketplace app (its events move across; reminders and member colours do not). Needs the Flock
  component library 1.3.0 (`expanded`, `InlinePanel`).
